A Dynamic Game-Theoretic Approach to Quantitative Finance: Nash Equilibria in Continuous-Time Markets

Authors: Huang Shan Chun, Martin Shubik
Volume: 8, Issue 3
Pages: 187-212
Publication Date: September 1985
Abstract: This paper presents a dynamic game-theoretic framework for modeling strategic interactions in continuous-time financial markets. We derive Nash equilibria for agents with exponential utility functions and analyze their stability in oligopolistic markets.
