About the Journal

Journal of Mathematical Finance and Game Theory is a peer-reviewed academic journal focusing on the intersection of mathematical finance and game theory. Founded in 1980, the journal publishes original research on dynamic models in financial markets.

Editor-in-Chief: Martin Shubik

ISSN: 0891-2345
